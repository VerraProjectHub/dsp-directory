          01 Overview

          Who this guide is for, key terms, and what you need before you start.

          Verra clients can access Guardian through two pathways (Section 02). Whichever pathway you choose, an active Verra Registry account is required before project data can be submitted to the Verra Project Hub.

          A digital submission involves two parties working in the same system: the project proponent (PP), who prepares the project documents, and the validation/verification body (VVB), who completes its validation and verification documents and responds to Verra’s findings. Both must be onboarded before a registration or verification request can move forward.

          ROLES AT A GLANCE

          Role

          What they do in Guardian / Project Hub

          Read sections

          Project proponent (incl. Account Holder - AH/Account Representative - AR)

          Registers in the policy, prepares the Project Description and monitoring reports, submits to Project Hub, pays all applicable Verra fees, and, onboard the VVB.

          01–04, 06–08

          Validation/verification body (VVB)

          Registers in the policy with the VVB role, reviews the PP created project documents including the audit findings on other aspect of the project to ensure adherence with all applicable VCS rules and requirements, completes validation/verification documents, and responds to Verra’s Project Review Report (PRR) findings.

          01–06, 08

          Verra Project Hub Support

          Grants access, links Hedera accounts, assigns policies, and approves PP and VVB roles in the policy.

          —

          Hashgraph

          Technical support for the Managed Guardian Service (MGS) platform used in Pathway 2. Providing necessary user login credentials to access Guardian services.

          GLOSSARY

          Term

          Meaning

          Hedera

          The underlying public distributed ledger (network) that records and secures data.

          Guardian

          The open-source MRV and tokenization platform, built on Hedera, that runs the digital methodologies.

          Verra Guardian Portal (VGP)

          Verra’s Managed Guardian Service – a hosted offering that lets organizations run Guardian without managing the underlying infrastructure.

          Managed Guardian Service (MGS)

          The hosted, cloud-based Guardian service. Pathway 2 users hold an MGS account and link it to Verra.

          Hedera Account ID

          Your account identifier on the Hedera network (format 0.0.xxxxxxx), shown on your MGS profile.

          DID

          Decentralized Identifier — the digital identity Guardian creates for each user, shown alongside the Hedera Account ID.

          Policy

          A methodology digitalized in Guardian (e.g., VM0050).

          PRR

          Project Review Report — Verra’s findings on a project review request for example registration, verification, crediting period renewal or re-quantification requests, to be responded by the VVB, if required in consultation with the project proponent.

          PREREQUISITES

          Project proponents

          VVBs

          • Active Verra Registry account. Register at registry.verra.org; Verra runs a KYC check.
          • USD 750 account opening fee, plus a USD 750 annual account maintenance fee.
          • Verra Project Hub account. Existing account holders can skip these steps.
          • Verra-approved VVB for the relevant sectoral scope and methodology.

          • Guardian access through one of the two pathways, then VVB Onboarding (Section 05).

          Why this matters: If the VVB isn’t onboarded, the PP’s registration or verification request has nowhere to go. Start VVB onboarding as soon as the PP selects a VVB, in parallel with the PP’s own onboarding.

          WHAT THE VVB DOES DURING THE LIFECYCLE

          • Registration and verification: complete the VVB documents for the request (e.g., validation/verification report) alongside the PP’s documents.
          • Deed of representation: create and submit the VVB’s deed of representation in Project Hub.
          • Project Review Report (PRR): Verra’s review team raises the PRR to the VVB, with the PP and AR in copy. The VVB responds to the findings in Project Hub; the PP has view-only access.

          07 Timelines & Fees

          Where to find current review times: Verra publishes target service level agreements (SLAs) and three-month rolling average processing times in the Project Tracker on Verra Project Hub. Technical review times increase by a factor of 1.25 for one complexity factor and 1.5 for two or more (for example, grouped project registration, methodology deviations, or use of two or more methodologies).
